Exchange rate: 1 USD = 3.6725 AED. AED values are rounded.

What a deck-builder changes against a standard budget for this scope (same total): more art (line 2: every card and enemy needs a face; +USD 1,800) and more balance testing (line 8: each scenario pack is a new deck and enemy set that has to be run through the balance check; +USD 600). Paid for by a smaller sound line (line 3: the sound effects and the music loop are already made in code; -USD 500), a trailer cut from gameplay capture (line 12; -USD 600) and a smaller contingency (line 16; -USD 1,300).

Developer time: the lead developer works about 80 hours a month on Payday Spire for all 12 months (line 1). Progress is shared in a monthly progress report and checked through the milestone deliverables.

Assumptions: contractor rates are market ranges (remote 2D artist USD 30/h, QA USD 20/h, Arabic localization USD 0.12 per word, WCAG audit USD 2,500). Shariah review, educator, sound and UAE travel costs are estimates to be confirmed by written quotes. Payment costs are included because award amounts are inclusive of bank and conversion costs.

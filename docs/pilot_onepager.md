# Payday Spire: a 4-week school pilot

A money lesson students play: their first working year in the UAE as a card battle. For schools, universities and bank or fintech youth and CSR programmes. Contact: [EMAIL] · Play: [ITCH_URL] · Code: [GITHUB_URL] · 2-minute video: [VIDEO_URL]

## The problem

Young people meet their first salary, first credit card and first pay-later offer at the same time. Card interest, an empty emergency fund and sale-driven spending are easy to explain and hard to feel. UAE banks already fund school financial-education sessions (for example Dubai Islamic Bank: about 50 schools, up to 4,000 students, 2023), but these programmes are often measured by attendance and satisfaction rather than by what students then do (The National, 16 Jan 2026).

## The solution

Twelve months, twelve short card battles. Bills, surprise costs, sales and last month's card bill are the enemies; money habits are the cards. Putting a bill on the card costs nothing today, then comes back next month as a card bill and lets Impulse buys into the deck. Students feel the rule before they read it.

## The 4-week pilot

| Week | In class | What the teacher sends us |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lesson 1 (45 min) with class code A (one goal for all: `?goal=`). Students play one year, then the class talk. | Results CSV from the dashboard. |
| 2 | Students replay the same year (class or home, about 15 min) and try to beat their last run. | Results CSV. |
| 3 | Lesson 2 with a new class code B, same goal: a new year, same rules. Does the habit carry over? | Results CSV and the pilot results page. |
| 4 | Wrap-up: each student writes one habit to keep. A 15-minute call with the teacher. | Five short teacher questions (time, fit, what to change). |

What the school needs: one class of 15 to 30 students aged about 15 to 22, a device per student, a projector, and 2 lessons plus one short replay.

## What we measure, and how

All numbers come from the dashboard's **Download results (CSV)** button: one row per student, no names, no device or account data.

| Measure | How it is read |
|---|---|
| Quick guesses, month 1 and year end | Two money questions after month 1, asked again with new numbers at year end: right answers per student, and the share who did better. |
| Card bill not paid in full | Share of students who left a card bill short at least once. Compared between code A and code B. |
| Emergency fund goal | Share who ended the year with a month of living costs set aside. |
| Interest and fees | Average paid, and share who paid none. |
| Grades and goal | Steady year / You made it / Card frozen; share who finished 12 months; share who reached their year goal (sample class: <!--n:sample_goalPct-->25<!--/n-->%). |
| Teacher fit | Minutes used, and the five teacher answers. |

Limits we state up front: two quick guesses are a sign, not a validated test. A fuller pre/post check is written with a financial-literacy educator in the funded plan (phase P2).

## Price (assumptions, not quotes)

- **Pilot:** free.
- **School licence after the pilot:** about AED 1,500 per school per year (assumption) for scenario packs, saved class reports and one code per class.
